**Project:** Fresco

**Hackathon:** Wema Bank Hackaholics 2026

---

## 1. What Is Fresco?

Fresco is a **commerce-to-finance platform for small-scale farmers**.

At the surface, Fresco is a marketplace that allows farmers to sell agricultural produce directly to nearby consumers. However, the marketplace is only the first layer of the product.

The deeper idea is that **every completed agricultural transaction creates evidence of real economic activity**. Fresco captures that activity, enriches it with AI-generated produce-quality information through Fresco, and converts the resulting history into a structured **Financial Passport**.

With the farmer's explicit consent, that Financial Passport can be shared with a financial institution such as Wema Bank. Wema can then use the verified business context as an additional source of information for customer acquisition, financial-product discovery, relationship management, and potential financing opportunities.

In simple terms:

> **Fresco helps a farmer sell produce today while building the financial identity that can help the farmer access better financial services tomorrow.**

---

# 2. The Problem Fresco Solves

Small-scale farmers often operate real businesses without having the kind of structured business records that formal financial institutions can easily understand.

A farmer may have:

- regular customers;
- strong sales;
- good-quality produce;
- reliable fulfillment;
- years of farming experience;
- growing revenue;
- increasing demand;

but much of that evidence may be scattered across cash transactions, bank transfers, notebooks, WhatsApp messages, market relationships, and informal records.

As a result, the farmer can be economically productive but financially difficult to evaluate.

At the same time, consumers may struggle to discover reliable nearby farmers, determine produce freshness, and purchase conveniently without physically visiting markets or farms.

Financial institutions also face a visibility problem: they may want to serve productive micro and small businesses but need structured information to understand business activity, consistency, revenue patterns, and operational reliability.

Fresco connects these three problems.

# 3. The Core Idea

Fresco is built around one simple chain:

```text
Agricultural Commerce
        ↓
Verified Economic Activity
        ↓
Structured Business History
        ↓
Financial Passport
        ↓
Farmer Consent
        ↓
```

The marketplace is therefore not the final product.

It is the **mechanism that generates trustworthy economic activity data**.

That is the key differentiator.

# 4. Fresco Has Four Connected Layers

## 4.1 Fresco Marketplace

The marketplace is the commerce layer.

Farmers can:

- create a profile;
- add produce;
- specify quantity and price;
- list produce for nearby consumers;
- receive orders;
- fulfill orders;
- receive reviews;
- track business activity.

Consumers can:

- discover nearby farmers;
- browse available produce;
- search and filter products;
- view produce quality information;
- place orders;
- make payments;
- track orders;
- review farmers after successful delivery.

The marketplace solves the immediate problem:

> **“How can a small farmer sell more easily to people nearby?”**

But every successful transaction also becomes valuable data for the second half of the platform.

---

# 5. Fresco — The AI Produce Intelligence Layer

**Fresco** is the produce-quality intelligence layer at the centre of the platform.

A farmer can upload or capture an image of produce, such as tomatoes, and Fresco analyzes the image to produce structured quality information.

For example:

```text
Produce: Tomato
Freshness: 91%
Estimated Shelf Life: 4 days
Confidence: 94%
Quality: Fresh
```

The result is attached to a produce batch or listing.

This creates a useful bridge between physical agriculture and digital commerce.

Instead of a marketplace simply saying:

> “Amaka is selling tomatoes.”

Fresco can say:

> “Amaka is selling tomatoes that were scanned by Fresco, with a recorded freshness estimate and shelf-life estimate.”

### Why Fresco matters

Fresco is not a standalone feature.

Its most important role is to create a **longitudinal quality history**.

For example:

```text
Batch 1 → Freshness 91%
Batch 2 → Freshness 88%
Batch 3 → Freshness 94%
Batch 4 → Freshness 92%
```

Fresco can then derive a quality-consistency metric for the farmer.

That quality history can contribute to the Financial Passport and also help consumers make better purchasing decisions.

---

# 6. The Financial Passport

The **Financial Passport** is the deeper product behind the marketplace.

It is a structured record of the farmer's verified economic activity on Fresco.

It can contain information such as:

- lifetime marketplace revenue;
- recent revenue;
- transaction count;
- completed orders;
- fulfillment rate;
- customer count;
- repeat customers;
- average customer rating;
- produce-quality consistency;
- active business duration;
- FEAP;
- profile maturity;
- business activity trajectory.

The important principle is:

> **The Financial Passport is generated from actual platform activity, not manually entered claims.**

For example, if a farmer completes another ₦25,000 transaction, the relevant business metrics can update automatically.

---

# 7. FEAP — Farmer Economic Activity Profile

FEAP stands for:

> **Farmer Economic Activity Profile**

FEAP is an **explainable activity indicator**, not a credit score.

It summarizes observed commercial activity using deterministic rules.

A prototype weighting can be:

| Component | Weight |
|---|---:|
| Transaction consistency | 30% |
| Revenue consistency | 20% |
| Fulfillment reliability | 15% |
| Customer reputation | 15% |
| Produce quality consistency | 10% |
| Business longevity | 10% |

Example:

> **FEAP: 78/100**

The farmer should also be able to see why the score is where it is.

For example:

```text
✓ Strong transaction consistency
✓ High fulfillment rate
✓ Strong customer reputation
✓ Consistent produce quality
△ Limited business history
```

FEAP does **not** approve or reject loans.

The purpose is to give a financial institution a structured view of economic activity that can complement its own assessment processes.

---

# 8. Profile Maturity

The Financial Passport also contains a **Profile Maturity** indicator.

Profile Maturity answers a different question from FEAP.

FEAP asks:

> “How strong and consistent is the farmer's observed economic activity?”

Profile Maturity asks:

> “How much verified business history has this farmer accumulated?”

Maturity can grow through signals such as:

- completed transactions;
- duration of activity;
- fulfillment history;
- customer reviews;
- quality scans;
- verified payment history;
- profile completeness.

Example:

```text
72% — Developing
85% — Established
95% — Mature
```

This gives farmers a clear reason to keep using the platform: their business history becomes more complete over time.

---

# 9. Consent Is a Core Feature

Fresco does not silently send a farmer's financial profile to Wema.

The farmer owns the sharing decision.

The farmer can see exactly what is being requested:

```text
Wema Bank wants access to:

✓ Transaction history
✓ Revenue/activity data
✓ Fulfillment history
✓ Produce quality history
✓ Customer reputation
✓ FEAP
✓ Profile maturity
```

The farmer chooses:

> **Allow Wema Access**

Once granted, the system records the consent and Wema can access the authorized profile.

This creates a **consent-based financial identity** rather than an invisible data-extraction system.

---

# 10. Why Wema Bank Matters

Wema Bank is the sponsor of the hackathon, so Fresco is intentionally designed to create a credible commercial relationship with Wema.

The key proposition is not:

> “Wema should use our marketplace instead of its existing banking products.”

It is:

> **“Fresco can become a new customer-acquisition, business-intelligence, and engagement channel that helps Wema understand productive agricultural businesses and connect them to appropriate financial services.”**

Wema already operates products and services relevant to businesses, payments, and SME financing. Fresco is designed to feed new business activity into that ecosystem rather than replace it.

# 12. The Wema Opportunity Engine

Fresco should not simply show information to Wema.

It should help identify **potential opportunities**.

For example:

```text
Amaka Farms

Opportunity Signal:
Working Capital

Why?
✓ 47+ completed transactions
✓ 96% fulfillment
✓ Growing recent revenue
✓ Strong repeat-customer activity

Suggested next step:
Explore Wema working-capital products.
```

Another farmer might receive:

> **Business Account Opportunity**

Another:

> **Payment Collection Opportunity**

Another:

> **Order Financing Opportunity**

These are **opportunity signals**, not guaranteed approvals.

The bank remains responsible for its actual customer onboarding, risk assessment, eligibility checks, and lending decisions.

---

# 13. Finance My Order

This is one of the most important features in the hackathon version.

Instead of waiting for a farmer to ask for a generic loan, Fresco can connect financing to actual commercial demand.

Imagine:

### Amaka receives a confirmed order

```text
Order Value: ₦500,000
```

But she needs working capital to produce, harvest, package, or deliver the order.

Fresco can show:

```text
Confirmed Order
₦500,000

Potential Working-Capital Need
₦150,000

[Explore Financing with Wema]
```

The request can then appear in the Wema dashboard.

The hackathon prototype does not need to perform real underwriting or disburse actual money. It needs to demonstrate the product journey and show where a real Wema integration would connect.

### Why this feature is powerful

It creates a direct loop:

```text
Confirmed demand
       ↓
Financing need
       ↓
Potential Wema financing
       ↓
Production / fulfillment
       ↓
Sale completed
       ↓
Revenue recorded
       ↓
Financial Passport becomes richer
```

This is much more compelling than simply saying that Fresco “helps farmers get loans.”

---

# 14. Farmer AI Growth Coach

Fresco can use AI to interpret the farmer's activity and provide business recommendations.

For example:

> “Your tomato demand has increased over the last three weeks. Your fulfillment rate is strong, but inventory availability is limiting completed orders. Consider increasing supply during peak demand periods.”

The AI can analyze:

- revenue trends;
- order frequency;
- fulfillment rate;
- repeat customers;
- quality consistency;
- produce categories;
- demand patterns.

The AI should explain the data rather than make binding financial decisions.

It can recommend actions, but it should never claim that a farmer is guaranteed a loan.

# 18. What Makes Fresco Different?

A normal agricultural marketplace says:

> “Farmers can sell their produce here.”

A freshness application says:

> “AI can inspect produce.”

A fintech lending application says:

> “We can assess business activity.”

Fresco combines all three into one economic loop:

```text
Produce
  ↓
Quality intelligence
  ↓
Commerce
  ↓
Verified transactions
  ↓
Financial identity
  ↓
Consent
  ↓
Financial services
```

The marketplace is the **data-generation mechanism**.

Fresco is the **quality-intelligence mechanism**.

The Financial Passport is the **identity mechanism**.

Wema is the **financial-services pathway**.

---

# 19. Why the Marketplace Is Important Even Though It Is Not the Whole Product

The marketplace gives Fresco a reason to collect real business events.

Without the marketplace, the Financial Passport would mostly depend on information a farmer enters manually.

With the marketplace, the system can observe:

- actual orders;
- actual payments within the platform;
- actual fulfillment;
- actual reviews;
- repeat purchases;
- actual produce listings;
- produce-quality scans.

Therefore:

> **Commerce creates the evidence.**

That evidence makes the financial identity more meaningful.

---

# 20. Why This Matters to Wema

From Wema's perspective, Fresco can potentially create several business opportunities.

### Customer acquisition

Fresco can introduce Wema to active agricultural micro-businesses.

### Payments

Marketplace activity can create opportunities to use banking and payment products.

### Business banking

Farmers who become more established can potentially be introduced to business-account products.

### Working capital

Farmers with strong observable commercial activity may become prospects for suitable financing products, subject to Wema's own assessment.

### Merchant relationships

As farmers grow, they can become recurring business customers rather than one-off borrowers.

### Data-informed engagement

Fresco can provide structured business context that helps Wema understand customer needs and identify relevant services.

The important pitch is not “Fresco replaces Wema's banking systems.”

The pitch is:

> **Fresco creates a new digital channel through which Wema can discover, understand, and serve agricultural micro-businesses.**

---

# 21. What Fresco Is Not

Fresco is **not**:

- a bank;
- a loan approval engine;
- a credit-score replacement;
- a guaranteed-loan platform;
- a complete logistics company;
- a replacement for Wema's core banking systems;
- a generic AI chatbot;
- merely a tomato marketplace;
- merely a freshness classifier.

These distinctions are important to keep the product credible.

# 23. The Technical Product Philosophy

Fresco is intentionally designed as a **modular monolith** for the 48-hour build.

The primary application can use:

- Next.js;
- TypeScript;
- Tailwind CSS;
- shadcn/ui;
- Framer Motion;
- PostgreSQL/Supabase;
- Prisma;
- Cloudinary;
- Fresco's existing AI service;
- OpenAI for business recommendations;
- Recharts for analytics;
- Vercel for deployment.

The core business logic should live in the application and database.

External systems such as payments, Fresco, and AI are accessed through provider interfaces so the prototype can degrade gracefully if an external service is unavailable.
